A planning aid, not an official program of study. This tool is here to explore possible paths through the doctoral program. Actual course offerings vary from year to year and depend on enrollment demand, faculty availability, and department needs — a course shown here may be rescheduled or not offered at all. Milestone timing (preliminary exam, prospectus, defense) depends on committee availability and the pace of the student’s own research, and cannot be scheduled from a course grid alone. Nothing here is a guarantee. Confirm the sequence with the major professor and the Graduate School’s current requirements before filing a program of study.
